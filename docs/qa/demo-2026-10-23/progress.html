TodayZ · build update

Foundation done. First screens are working.

TodayZ lets neighbourhood shops put their stock online so people nearby can find an item, book it and pick it up. This page covers what is built, what you can see today and the time left to a tested product.

Friday, 23 October 2026 Everything runs locally; nothing is deployed yet

What we can show today

Business app

The shop owner's dashboard

Today's sales against last week, new orders with a countdown to accept, demand insights such as what sells fast and what is about to expire, and top items. Desktop and phone. It runs on sample data and is labelled that way.

Website

The new marketing site

Home, for retailers, pricing, how it works, contact and the legal pages, with a lead form for shops. English is final; Hindi, Kannada, Telugu and Tamil are machine drafts awaiting native review.

Design system

32 reusable components

Product and store cards, the map with nearby shops, the till cart, pickup codes, order timeline and dashboard tiles. Every app is built from these, so screens look the same everywhere.

Every change is reviewed and tested before it joins the main build. No real customer data is used anywhere; all screens show sample shops and products.

Not real yet

  • No login, orders or payments. Those arrive in stages 1 and 2.
  • The dashboard shows sample numbers. Its buttons show a notice and change nothing.
  • Indian-language copy is machine translated and needs native review before launch.
  • Nothing is online yet. The site and apps run on the build machine only.

Decisions we need

  • Legal sign-off on the privacy and terms pages, and a named grievance officer.
  • Native reviewers for Hindi, Kannada, Telugu and Tamil.
  • The old website's page list, so links keep working after the switch.
  • Cloud accounts (AWS and Cloudflare) when we reach stage 5.

Timeline

Revised estimate

The first target was stages 0 to 4 within a day. That was not realistic: stage 0 alone took about a day and a half, because each piece is reviewed and fixed before the next one builds on it.

At the current pace, a fully tested product running locally (end of stage 4) is about 3 to 4 weeks away, with launch in the cloud about a week after that.
