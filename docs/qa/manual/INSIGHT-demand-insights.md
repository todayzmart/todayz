# INSIGHT Demand insights

The Demand insights card on the shop's Overview and its Insights tab in Analytics (D-99): eleven kinds, each with one sentence, one action that opens its screen prefilled and applies nothing, a severity, a "Not useful" button (except Expired on shelf) and a minimum of evidence. The tester reads every sentence against the seed, stands on both sides of each boundary, opens every action without saving, then saves a few on purpose, changes the settings, checks who may see the card, and looks for anything that names another shop or a customer.

- **Covers:** SC-IN; IN-01 to IN-21 and IN-24 to IN-27 (IN-22, IN-23 and IN-28 are automated only, see [traceability.md](traceability.md)); AI-R4 (wording only); R-12.8, R-16.4, R-16.5; D-71, D-81, D-99; Q-52 (the "Priced above nearby" kind stays off until the founder decides).
- **Screens:** B-03, B-05, B-07, B-08, B-09, B-12; C-07; A-03, A-17.
- **Devices:** shop D1280 (profile B: O20, M21, X25; profile C: M24, K22, M29), customer P360 (profile A: C10), admin D1440 (profile D: AD47, SA48).
- **Time:** about 3.5 hours.

## Preconditions

1. `make reset`; `clock set 2026-10-22T11:00:00+05:30` (a Thursday), the clock time the seed stamps its items at. Every date below counts from it. Every window is whole IST days ending yesterday, so the counts hold all day. The weekday in the Selling fast sentence does not: Tata Tea Gold runs out 1.31 days (31.5 hours) from now, so the sentence says "Friday" only up to 16:30 IST and flips to "Saturday" after it. If you take a break that runs past 16:30 IST, `clock set` 11:00 again before you carry on.
2. The seeds `<seed:history-SL>`, `<seed:wanted-nearby>`, `<seed:insights-SL>`, `<seed:insights-SL2>` and `<seed:price-compare>` are loaded, and the rollups ran at the end of the seed (the search demand and the pair counts exist). If the Network panel shows an empty `items` list for Sri Lakshmi 2 in step INSIGHT-M.08, stop and mark the script **B**.
3. Every `insight_*` key is at its 01 §17 default, and `stock_stale_after_hours` is 72. `insight_price_compare_enabled` is false. `ai_insighttext_enabled` is false, so the sentences below are the plain templates (step INSIGHT-M.42 turns the AI wording on).
4. AD47 has enrolled TOTP (MQ-07.01 to MQ-07.07). If not, mark the AD47 parts of INSIGHT-M.23, INSIGHT-M.32 and INSIGHT-M.43 **B**.
5. The app prints the product name in bold. This book does the same with **bold**.
6. The card holds only kinds that fire, at most one row per kind, four rows at most. The order is fixed: all `high` rows, then `medium`, then `low`; inside a severity the kinds go in this order: Selling fast, Sitting on the shelf, Going to waste, Wanted nearby, Missed sales, Expired on shelf, Selling below cost, Stock check due, Bought together, Regulars slipping away, Priced above nearby (01 §12). Rows of one kind go by the money or units at stake, larger first. Write down any order that differs.
7. Sections A to F save nothing. Before each "nothing saved" check, note the item's on hand in B-07 → history.
8. Dates and counts for Sri Lakshmi 2 come from the item table in [README.md](README.md) §6 (J1 to J22).
