# ASK Ask shops nearby and search proof

Ask shops nearby, and the proof on search results (D-101). A customer who cannot find an item asks the shops near them, and each shop answers Yes or No in one tap from the business app. The customer sees live progress and books a Yes at the replied price. Search results show the proof: a summary line, badges, "Confirmed … ago", refine chips, an optional mic and Share. The admin console lists unanswered demand and approves the marketing site's search strip. The tester checks every number against the seed, stands on both sides of the reply window and the price window, and reads the Network panel for anything that could identify the customer.

- **Covers:** SC-ASK; ASK-01 to ASK-19 (ASK-20 is automated only, see [traceability.md](traceability.md); ASK-08's item-offer case and ASK-06's `403` on a cashier's price are automated too); E2E-21 (sections D to F are its manual run); AI-C5; R-11.8 to R-11.26, R-15.10, R-15.11, R-16.7 to R-16.10, R-17.5; D-101; Q-53 to Q-57 (the plan's defaults until the founder decides) and Q-58 (for the lead).
- **Screens:** C-05, C-07, C-08, C-10, C-11, C-23, C-25, C-26; B-04, B-07, B-14, B-20; A-13, A-17, A-21, A-23; S-01, S-07.
- **Devices:** customer P360 (profile A: C10, C15, C51–C55; profile E, signed out), shop D1280 (profile B: X25, O20, O26, O27, M21; profile C: M24, K22), admin D1440 (profile D: SA48, G43, Y44, F40, S42, FN45), site D1280.
- **Time:** about 3 hours.

## Preconditions

1. `make reset`; `clock set 2026-10-22T11:00:00+05:30` (a Thursday). The seed stamps its asks relative to this time. Every shop in Bengaluru is open (NH opens at 10:00).
2. The seeds `<seed:asks>`, `<seed:ask-gaps>` and `<seed:site-marquee>` are loaded (README §7), on top of `<seed:customers>`, `<seed:staff>`, `<seed:admins>`, `<seed:stores>` and `<seed:items-SL>`.
3. Every key in the 01 §17 "Asks and search proof" table is at its default: `ask_daily_limit_customer` 5, `ask_radius_m` 3000, `ask_max_stores` 8, `ask_daily_limit_store` 40, `ask_reply_window_min` 15, `ask_price_valid_min` 60, `ask_low_yield_after_count` 20, `ask_low_yield_max_stores` 3, `ask_gap_min_customers` 10, `ask_gap_window_days` 7, `site_marquee_min_searchers` 10, `site_marquee_window_days` 7, `site_marquee_max_items` 20, `refine_ai_min_words` 4, `ask_retention_days` 90. `ask_whatsapp_enabled` and `ai_voicesearch_enabled` are false. `stock_stale_after_hours` is 72.
4. Mocks in fixture mode, as MQ-11. `make env-check` may show `SARVAM_API_KEY missing`: in `local` the speech-to-text mock counts as configured (11 §1 rule 2). If the mic still does not show after ASK-M.20 turns `ai_voicesearch_enabled` on, mark ASK-M.20 to ASK-M.24 **B**.
5. Run section A first, within 10 minutes of the clock set: C55's seeded ask closes at 11:13. If more time has passed, `clock set 2026-10-22T11:00:00+05:30` again before ASK-M.01.
6. Every customer step uses location QA point (README §6). Searches use radius 2 km unless the step says otherwise. Asks always use `ask_radius_m` (3 km), whatever radius the customer picked.
7. At the QA point these four stores are open, `live`, verified and within 3 km: SL (0.6 km), NH (0.9 km), AP (1.2 km), SL2 (1.8 km). KF (3.5 km) and AM (4.2 km) are outside `ask_radius_m`. The six non-live stores PS, DS, PR, SS, NI and SB never get an ask. NH is In-store only.
8. Asks are a privacy feature. In every Network check, record the field names and status only (README §5). A step fails if any shop-side response, push or stream event carries the customer's name, phone, user id, address, coordinates or a distance in metres.
