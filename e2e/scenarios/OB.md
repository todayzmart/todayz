# Observability scenarios (SC-OB)

OB-01…OB-06 are fixed in [08-testing.md](../../docs/plan/08-testing.md) §3 and belong to W1-07 (and W1-11 for OB-05). OB-11…OB-30 belong to W3-07 (`backend/internal/rollups`, `internal/jobs/rollups.go`, `infra/grafana/dashboards/{00,05..13}`, `infra/grafana/alerts/`, `make demo`); OB-11 is fixed in 08 §3, the rest are added here. Format as in 08 §3: `ID · Given / When / Then · level` (U unit, I integration, E E2E, M manual).

| ID | Scenario | Lv | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| OB-11 | Given a seeded IST day of orders, POS sales, searches, events and stores, when the day's rollups are computed, then every platform, store and demand count equals the raw tables' count for that day, and equals the fixture's own numbers | I, M | `backend/internal/rollups/rollups_test.go` `TestRollupsMatchRawCountsForASeededDay`; PERF-M.16, ACC-41 |
| OB-12 | Given a computed day, when it is computed again, or an offline POS sale for that day syncs late and the day is recomputed, then there is still one row per day and the late sale is counted | I | `rollups_test.go` `TestRecomputeIsIdempotentAndPicksUpLateRows` |
| OB-13 | Given the clock inside the day being computed, when the rollup runs, then the row is not final and its `computed_at` is the injected clock (the test clock locally), never the wall clock | I | `rollups_test.go` `TestTodayIsPartialAndUsesTheClock` |
| OB-14 | Given 00:30 IST on 20 Oct (19:00 UTC on the 19th), when it is mapped to a day, then it is 20 Oct, which runs 18:30 UTC to 18:30 UTC | U | `rollups_test.go` `TestDayHelpers` |
