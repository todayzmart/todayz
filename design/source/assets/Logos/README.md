# Logos

- `todayz-logo.jpg` is the primary logo: the green bag with the Z and the TodayZ wordmark. Use it on light grounds (`bg` or `surface`).
- `todayz-logo-alt.jpg` is the alternate lockup, for print, decks and social banners.
- `todayz-bag-mark.png` is the bag alone. It is the source for the app icon, PWA icons and favicon; at 48px and below use only this mark.
- The mark's green is `brand-mark` (#078B44) and its wordmark ink is #0C231F (close to `ink`). Never recolour, outline or redraw it.
- These are JPEG/PNG on white. Vector (SVG) and reversed (white-on-black) versions are still needed before launch.
